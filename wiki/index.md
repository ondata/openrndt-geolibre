# openrndt-geolibre

* [openrndt-geolibre](overview.md) - A GeoLibre plugin to search the Italian national catalogue of spatial data (RNDT) and add its services to the map.

# Sections

* [Guides](guides/) - How to install the plugin, search, add layers and share a search.
* [Reference](reference/) - The query, the URL parameters, the project state, coordinate systems, layer names, footprints, GeoLibre hooks, settings.
* [Limits](limits/) - What the plugin, or GeoLibre around it, cannot do, and the way around when there is one.
* [Decisions](decisions/) - Choices made in the design of the plugin, with their reasons.
* [Development](development/) - Build, test, release, and how to try a build in GeoLibre.
* [Upstream](upstream/) - What is asked of GeoLibre and what the plugin registry serves.
